# Translate Mystery Box Builder

## What it does
Mystery Box Builder lets you translate every piece of customer-facing text — button labels, titles, and more — into any language your store supports. It also ships with ready-made Japanese text, so setting up Japanese takes a couple of clicks instead of typing every field by hand.

## 1. Edit the default text

![Edit the default text](../img/01-default-text-annotated.png)

Every piece of customer-facing text in Mystery Box Builder — button labels, titles, and more — starts from a default value you can edit directly, no matter which language your store uses. From your Shopify admin, open **Mystery Box Builder**, then **Content & Translation** from the sidebar, on the **Default Text** tab:

1. Edit any field — for example, update **Box items title**.
2. Click **Save**.

The app also ships with ready-made Japanese text: in the bottom-right corner of the main Mystery Box Builder dashboard, switch the **Language** dropdown to **Japanese** and every default field above pre-fills automatically — that's why the fields in the screenshot are already in Japanese instead of English.

> **Note:** Only edit the text outside the curly braces `{}` — those hold dynamic values the app fills in automatically.

## 2. Translate the app into your store's other languages

![Translate the app into your store's other languages](../img/02-translation-french-annotated.png)

Switch to the **Translation** tab.

1. Next to **English Default**, use the language picker to choose one of the other languages your store is set up in — here, **French**.
2. For each field, type your translation into the box on the right; the English text on the left is shown only for reference.
3. Click **Save** to publish the translation.

Repeat for any other language your store supports.
