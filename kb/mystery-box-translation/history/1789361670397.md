# Translate Mystery Box Builder

## What it does
Mystery Box Builder lets you translate every piece of customer-facing text — button labels, titles, and more — into any language your store supports. It also ships with ready-made Japanese text, so setting up Japanese takes one click instead of typing every field by hand.

## 1. Switch the app language to Japanese

![Switch the app language to Japanese](../img/01-dashboard-language-annotated.png)

By default, Mystery Box Builder displays all its text in English. To use the built-in Japanese text instead:

1. From your Shopify admin, open **Mystery Box Builder**.
2. In the bottom-right corner of the dashboard, open the **Language** dropdown and select **Japanese**.

The app's interface switches to Japanese right away — there's nothing to save here.

## 2. Default text fields are now pre-filled in Japanese

![Default text fields are now pre-filled in Japanese](../img/02-default-text-japanese-annotated.png)

Open **Content & Translation** from the sidebar, on the **Default Text** tab. Every field is now pre-filled with Japanese text instead of the original English — no typing required.

1. Change any field to apply the update — for example, edit **Box items title**.
2. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** Only edit the text outside the curly braces `{}` — those hold dynamic values the app fills in automatically.

## 3. Translate the app into your store's other languages

![Translate the app into your store's other languages](../img/03-translation-french-annotated.png)

Switch to the **Translation** tab. Next to **English Default**, use the language picker to choose one of the other languages your store is set up in — here, **French**.

1. For each field, type your translation into the box on the right; the English text on the left is shown only for reference.
2. Click **Save** to publish the translation.

Repeat for any other language your store supports.
